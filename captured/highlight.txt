  Note what the agent never did: it never edited a rendered manifest
  to make a check go green. Every fix changed an input and the whole
  artefact was regenerated. Patch the output instead and the finding
  comes back silently on the next render — that is how automated
  remediation becomes worse than none.

  The agent is not the platform.

  Act II is what an agent does with a model and no platform: 42 ways
  to be plausibly, confidently wrong. The only thing that changed by
  Act V was the platform around it — a golden path, a provisioner set,
  a policy bundle, and an authorization model that treats an agent as
  a principal with a blast radius.

  DORA’s 2025 finding, in one line: where platform quality is low, the
  effect of AI adoption on organisational performance is negligible.

  Agentic platform engineering is not AI replacing platform engineers.
  It is platform engineers becoming the thing that decides whether
  any of this is safe to turn on.
